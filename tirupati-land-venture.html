Project briefing

Turn our 2.5 acres into a subsidised green manufacturing unit

Proposal for a women-led MSME making biomass pellets near Tirupati — B2B, bio-friendly, and aligned to state + central subsidy schemes.

Non-torrefied biomass pellet plant on our existing agri land.

Capital plan: ₹2–3 Cr · Local residue in · Industrial fuel out

What this site is best at

Moving bulk raw material in and bulk finished goods out. That favours B2B manufacturing over pure retail or plot-sale thinking.

Shared objective

Company first, conversion done properly, subsidies stacked

What we want

  • Real manufacturing MSME (not a paper company)
  • Bio-friendly B2B product with local demand
  • Maximum eligible state + central incentives

What we are not doing

Recommendation

Primary business: biomass pellets

Compress agri waste (groundnut shell, maize/cotton stalk, husk, sawdust) into industrial fuel pellets sold to boilers and institutions.

Why pellets

Best match for our road access, capital size, local residue belt, and subsidy stack.

Why B2B

Recurring industrial offtake beats one-time retail. Easier to scale with trucks from an NH-linked site.

Why bio

Waste-to-fuel replaces coal/wood heat. Policy tailwinds from MNRE and AP green industry push.

  • Maize / cotton stalk residues
  • Rice husk & sawdust via NH logistics
  • Need covered storage + dryer for moisture control

Areca leaf packaging stays interesting for Tirupati hotels, but feedstock is weaker locally than agri residue for pellets.

Ownership structure

Women-led company can unlock higher AP support

AP MSME Policy 4.0 angle

  • Eligible manufacturing MSMEs can claim capital subsidy on fixed capital investment
  • Special category (incl. women-owned, with domicile rules) can get enhanced capital subsidy
  • 100% reimbursement of land conversion charges (if eligible)
  • Power / SGST style reimbursements may also apply

How we should structure it

  • Private Limited with clear manufacturing objects
  • Women ownership structured genuinely (not cosmetic)
  • Udyam + GST + factory / PCB compliances
  • Incentives claimed after commercial production — plan cash accordingly
Important

Subsidies are reimbursements for a real running plant. They improve ROI — they do not replace the need for buyer contracts and working capital.

Returns

ROI is strong when offtake is locked first

Plant scale

Target a 2–3 TPH non-torrefied pellet line on part of the 2.5 acres; keep buffer for storage/expansion.

Return profile

Industry references for well-run mid-scale plants often point to roughly 18–30 month payback — only with utilisation and contracts.

Margin killers

Wet residue, high power cost, quality rejects, and weak buyer prices. These matter more than brochure subsidy numbers.

Decision rule

Do not order machines until we have residue supplier terms + at least 2–3 serious buyer conversations / LOIs.

Land & compliance

Convert properly — then claim what policy allows

1

Keep use honest. Manufacturing on agri land needs lawful conversion / planning clearances as applicable in AP.

2

Use MSME pathway. Eligible industrial use can claim conversion-fee reimbursement under AP MSME 4.0 — reimbursement, not evasion.

3

Parallel approvals. Factory licence, APPCB consent if required, GST, Udyam, and BioUrja / nodal filings for MNRE CFA.

4

Protect the asset. Clean compliance keeps the land financeable and sellable.
